---
name: mars-bug-triage
description: Turn a bug report into a reproducible, testable engineering issue.
---

Capture expected behavior, observed behavior, affected version, and the smallest ordinary input that demonstrates the problem. Separate reporter observations from your hypotheses. If information is missing, ask for the one detail most likely to distinguish the causes.

Identify the first observable divergence, not just the final error. Propose a regression assertion at that boundary and state what would disprove the leading hypothesis. Do not invent reproduction steps or claim to have run them.
